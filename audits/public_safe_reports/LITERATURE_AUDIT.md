# Q1718 literature review

Checked on November 28, 2026.

## Exact question and source chronology

- Q1718 asks whether an element of `Homeo¹(S)` can have an uncountable set of nondifferentiability points or one of positive area. Here both the map and its inverse preserve embedded regular C¹ circles as subsets of the surface. Differentiability is Fréchet differentiability in local smooth coordinates. [Catalog entry and setup](https://github.com/10m-u/open-problems-catalog/blob/main/problems/mathematics/topology-part-1.md#q1718)
- Katherine Williams Booth's 2025 Georgia Tech dissertation explicitly asks both questions in §1.6, Question 3, printed page 7 (PDF page 20). The most recent arXiv version of her local characterization, v4 dated October 7, 2026, asks them again as Question 1.5, printed/PDF page 3, and explains the potential interference among nearby singularities. The November 11, 2025 v3 already includes both questions in §1.3, Question 1. The arXiv submission's October 28, 2024 date is the date of v1 rather than the verified date of this question. [Dissertation item](https://hdl.handle.net/1853/77809), [dissertation PDF](https://repository.gatech.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/1518c5d1-9c3b-4271-ba33-acf4f5a41247/content#page=20), [arXiv v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.21460v3#page=4), [arXiv v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.21460v4#page=3), [arXiv version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21460)

## Closest prior work and contribution

- Frédéric Le Roux and Maxime Wolff give a single-point nondifferentiability example in §5 of *Automorphisms of some variants of fine graphs*. Booth's v4 Lemma 2.1 already gives a point of nondifferentiability at which the induced tangent direction map is the identity. Booth's Lemma 2.4 patches single-point examples on disjoint supports around `(2⁻ⁿ,0)`, with distortion diminishing at their accumulation point. Booth additionally remarks that her setup can make that one limit point singular. The qualitative idea that tangent information can survive a failure of ambient differentiability is therefore already present in the literature. [Le Roux–Wolff, §5](https://msp.org/agt/2024/24-8/agt-v24-n8-p16-s.pdf), [Booth, Lemma 2.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.21460v4#page=5), [Booth, Lemma 2.4 and following remark](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.21460v4#page=8)
- The candidate's contribution on the uncountability branch is the construction of a disk-supported, locally bi-Lipschitz surface homeomorphism with an exceptional set exactly a Cantor set and two-sided preservation of regular C¹ circles. Nested radial patches confine all nonscalar derivative distortion to the annulus where a point first exits the nested cores. The annular anisotropy tends uniformly to zero near each Cantor point, while the cumulative scalar factor alternates between 1 and 2. Thus the proof must show that every point of a perfect set is itself singular, including all accumulation points.
- The positive-area exclusion applies the Gehring–Lehto 1959 theorem for continuous open planar maps with finite first partial derivatives almost everywhere. Menchoff obtained the planar homeomorphism case in 1931. Under Booth's hypothesis, each coordinate segment lies in a test circle, and the image of the segment is locally a C¹ graph traversed with a monotone scalar coordinate. One-variable differentiability of monotone functions and Fubini give the required finite partials. This application uses only forward curve preservation. [Gehring–Lehto bibliography](https://data.nationallibrary.fi/bib/me/W00551526000), [published formulation in Theorem 1](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/2014/895074), [Menchoff's paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01455809), [National Academy of Sciences attribution](https://www.nasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Gehring_Frederick.pdf)
