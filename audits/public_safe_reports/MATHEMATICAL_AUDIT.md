# Mathematical audit

The complete proof distinguishes regularity of an image subset from regularity with respect to an inherited parameter. For the area branch, monotonicity on subarcs gives finite almost-everywhere derivatives on each segment. The homeomorphism is open, so the planar differentiability theorem applies in charts.

For the construction, the first exit from nested cores leaves at most one nonscalar derivative factor. Its error tends uniformly to zero deeper in the construction. The lower bilipschitz bound follows by integrating the longitudinal component, and inverse secant control follows on each common image core. The graph argument proves both existence and continuity of its derivative. At every binary address the vertical test points avoid the child supports, and the errors in the two subsequences tend to zero. The independent finite replay addresses only the scale estimates.
