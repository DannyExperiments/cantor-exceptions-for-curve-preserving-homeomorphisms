One Cantor address, two limiting scales

Each fork chooses a left or right child disk. Depth runs from the root to the last row. Height is the exact dilation of that core at its finite stage. Drag the rotation control to see a single path climb and fall.

Teal points have scale 1. Gold points have scale 2. Horizontal spacing is schematic. For the actual map at a limit point, the vertical difference quotient stays within 2−n−8 of the stage-n scalar times the vertical unit vector. In the plane the core disks lie on a common axis.
