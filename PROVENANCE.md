# Provenance

This project develops catalog Q1718 from a preserved proof packet dated November 27, 2026. The original question appears in Booth's dissertation and Question 1.5 of her fourth arXiv version. The manuscript presents the same nested-disk construction, supplies the explicit quotient error and deduces inverse angular control by taking preimages in a common image core.
